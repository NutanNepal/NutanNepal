I am a Mathematics Ph.D. candidate at **North Carolina State University**, advised by [Dr. Jacob P. Matherne](https://jpmather.math.ncsu.edu/research.html). 

My research centers on **Combinatorial Hodge Theory**, **Matroid Theory**, and **Incidence Algebras of Posets** — particularly Lorentzian polynomials, Kazhdan–Lusztig–Stanley (KLS) theory, and Chow rings of combinatorial geometries. Alongside pen-and-paper math, I build computational research tooling and formalize mathematical proofs in **Lean 4**.

### 🔬 Featured Research Repositories
